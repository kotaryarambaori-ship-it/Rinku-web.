PP Woven Sack Manufacturing Industry

A simple overview of how polypropylene woven sacks are commonly made. Processes differ between factories.

  1. Polypropylene (PP) granules are melted and made into thin flat tapes.
  2. Weaving: the tapes are woven on looms into polypropylene woven fabric.
  3. Fabric: the fabric can be coated, printed or left plain depending on the use.
  4. Sack making: the fabric is cut and stitched into woven sacks and bags.
  5. Quality checks look at size, strength, stitching and appearance.
  6. Packaging use: the sacks pack grain, flour, fertilizer, cement, sugar and other goods for industrial packaging.
